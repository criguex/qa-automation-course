## Description

A spec said "weekly window" and its example lasted 38 days. In this module of my QA course I review a specification before anyone builds it: I recompute every number with a script, rate severity, turn blockers into questions, and split the build into gated phases. Then I test the reviewer with mutants. Using the public, anonymized spec-review-case-study.
