## Title
Mutation testing: is your green suite asleep?

## Description
A golden flow that's green end to end, and at the same time an editor who promotes themself to admin while the app answers 200. In this video I show you how to break something on purpose to find out whether your suite is awake: the four WAR steps, the PASS, KILLED and SURVIVED verdicts, a kill rate that leaves out the baseline and infrastructure, the survivor ledger, and the classic tools, Stryker and PIT. Real lab with war-paradigm (experiment 02), no keys needed.

Module 20 of the QA Automation course (m15 · Mutation testing).
