# Metadata · Module 8b (EN)

## Title
QA sign-off: certifying a story, and why a human signs

## Description
The golden flow was green. If I'd signed off on that green, I would have certified a privilege escalation bug that only a mutant found. In this module you close the method: a certification Definition of Done, real links with no placeholders, self-contained evidence that outlives CI, the errors that invalidate a sign-off, and one mutant per requirement before you sign. With war-paradigm (story US-42, a QA vault and mutants), restful-booker-quality-suite and spendwise-qa-assessment.
